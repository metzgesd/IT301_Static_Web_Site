Accidents happen...

As should be done with any prospective hobby or interest, always know your risk before you get into it. In the case of cars, there is unfortunately a very real physical danger when moving quick in something that weighs over a ton. There is also a large financial risk too, cars are expensive not only to buy but to repair as well, especially when something goes really wrong. This is just going to be a quick summary of two of my worst incidents. Please enjoy.

The Cruise of Doom and Despair

With the car club here at JMU, Madison Motorsports, we often take our cars out on cruises where we engage in some spirited driving on rural mountain roads. It's fun, it's safe, and it gives us an excuse to hang out. On one fateful cruise however, the route was mixed up and ended sending us deep into West Virginia for several hours. I had just gotten my car and was very excited to be driving it, probably driving it a little harder than I should've. The excessive vibration of the engine led to a bolt wiggling out and a vital belt shredding as a result. This left my new precious car stuck in a lady's front yard. She was very nice about the whole thing though and even after I recovered the car the next day, we've stayed in contact since.

Stuck in Jersey

At one point over winter break, I took my Integra all the way up to the glorious state of New Jersey to visit a friend across the state line in New York City. Let me simply say this. The roads of Jersey City are not made for regular vehicles. A series of potholes and railroad tracks ended up shredding one of the tires my car came with and there was a very real possibility I would be stuck in Jeresey until I could find a tire to put on the thankfully intact rim. Luckily, a random tire shop across the city had the strange old tire size I needed and I was able to get out of there as soon as I was done visiting.
